SKETS Studio CSR · Since 2019

Free education and real-world skills for East Delhi’s children, women & youth.

The SKETS Hope Centre removes financial barriers so people from underprivileged communities can learn, earn and become self-reliant. Fully funded by SKETS Studio, run with the Shanti Devi Mittal Foundation.

What we offer — all free

Programs

Computer Classes

Four batches of 15 students, each with a dedicated computer for individual learning.

Coaching Classes

Experienced teachers conduct daily classes in various subjects for over 250 students.

Beauty Classes

Training in beauty and makeup skills to empower young women and widen their opportunities.

Self Defence

Training women and children in self-defence to protect themselves and others.

🧵

Stitching Classes

Morning and evening classes teaching young women and girls sewing and stitching.

💉

Covid-19 Support

Vaccinated 300+ people and distributed rations, masks and sanitizers during the pandemic.
